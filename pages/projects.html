Ruby Rose

Solo

2024

Ruby Rose is a character from the RWBY series. I implemented a variety of moves for her, the major ones being techniques she used in the show like the gun on her scythe and actually integrated her semblance in the character which wasn't available in the previous version. This project was a favorite of the ones I released because unlike my prior MUGEN releases, I experimented with the AI a lot more and made her favor some actions over others depending on the situation. While not a scoring system which is what I use today, this project marks the turning point in how I approached AI development in general.
