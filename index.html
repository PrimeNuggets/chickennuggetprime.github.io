Urban Engines: Baltimore Drift

Team | Unity | 95% code contribution | Group 2D

2025

This game is the first project where I worked collaboratively with others (4 others specifically) to create a game. Furthermore, this game was created in response to B-360's request for a game that was both educational and fun.
Though this was a group project, the rest of my team was art focused. This left me in charge of most of the code behind the game. The main challenge with this game was surprisingly the physics of the side-scroller (fun) section. In past projects, I would normally have a character that would stop the instant the forward/backward control wasn't being pressed however for this game, we wanted the bike keep moving if the player stopped pressing a directional control.

About Me

I am an aspiring Systems Designer with a strong programming passion for creating engaging and immersive experiences. Most of my public projects are school-related with the exception of MUGEN content I create as a hobby. My public MUGEN content is published primarily on my MugenArchive account and Patreon that currently has a follower count of 33. Though my following is less on MugenArchive, my reach is a lot greater in the community.

A lot of what I focus on in my designs is combat systems, i.e.: entity stats, leveling mechanics, pathfinding AI, combat AI, etc. These systems are my preference to manage because I love to see my creations not only move from point A to point B. But to do so with a purpose.
One other major thing I focus on is the design of the system itself. Things like the UI, physics, controls, etc. While less of a preference, I do like to spend a large amount of time making sure the framework of my game feels smooth or simple for a player to manage.
